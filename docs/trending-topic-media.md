# Trending topic media previews

Search, All Topics and the timeline topic carousel share a small decorative preview from actual entries in the topic's supplied feed. Up to three distinct posts contribute direct image thumbnails or an available video still. Gallery images are supported; external cards and quoted media are omitted. Topic buttons retain their existing routes. Catbird's expanded variable-width title font wraps fully; Search moves artwork below the title at accessibility sizes. Decorative images and participant circles stay outside VoiceOver navigation; Reduce Motion removes rotation.

The account-owned raw cache admits two feed requests at a time and coalesces requests for the same link. New requests fetch twelve posts, retain at most thirty raw entries per topic, and cache at most twenty topics for five minutes; failures use a one-minute negative cache. An existing default-labeler prefetched feed may be reused once per link, for at most twenty links. Later refreshes use fresh authenticated feed responses. Graph changes and foreground activation discard preview entries and disable prefetched reuse. Cancellation and stale completions cannot refill invalidated entries.

Moderation runs before selecting image or avatar URLs on every render, including cache hits. It checks active post/author labels, self-labels, muted/blocked actors and lists, hidden posts, muted threads, muted words including alt text and tags, quick filters, language and feed/reply preferences. Unhydrated or unsafe reply contexts and quoted contexts are omitted. All active labels, including informational or unknown custom labels, receive text fallback because these decorative cards have no warning/reveal interaction. Negated and expired labels are inactive according to Catbird's existing label helper. Local labeler subscriptions must exactly match the applied accepted-labeler header, including Bluesky's default labeler. A pending or failed header update suppresses previews. All Topics participant navigation uses the same freshly evaluated feed-derived profiles as the preview, rather than cached trend-metadata actors with unknown labeler provenance. Small resized Nuke requests use Catbird's shared pipeline and CDN avatar thumbnails.

This topic starts from installed integrated Lite revision `63ba77248cf5e3801d689e8f350649e74d0fad07`; it does not modify the dirty Lite checkout or drawer/banner work. Petrel and the Catbird overlay remain the integration owner's frozen revisions. The moderation audit found direct feed-menu mute/block calls that bypassed graph caches; those actions now use GraphManager. Thread mute actions emit graph-change signals, including notification settings. Foreground refresh covers moderation performed in background App Intents or other clients. A concurrent remote moderation change while Catbird remains foreground can remain stale until the next bounded cache refresh; this is not a real-time moderation subscription.

Validation uses focused typed protocol fixtures, standalone simulator Swift Testing, and clearly labeled production-view component fixtures with a saved public feed snapshot. Public endpoint samples verify media availability but contain no authenticated viewer state, so they cannot qualify real-user moderation. Full app compilation/runtime acceptance is separately coordinated through the integration owner's slot ledger. Source/runner manifests, exact results, screenshots and retained limits live in the task's `evidence` directory; no phone operations or real account writes are part of this task.
